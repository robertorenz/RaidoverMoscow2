The Raid

  1. Hangar Launch — throttle up, lift off and thread the bay door as it cycles.
  2. Low-Level Flight — stay under the red radar floor. Climb too long and MiGs scramble and SAMs launch. Watch for trees, power lines and tower blocks.
  3. Launch Site Strike — the Control Center's roof hatches are only vulnerable while open. Shoot rising ICBMs before they launch, or a US city is lost.
  4. Red Square — set rocket range with ↑/↓ and blast the five bunker doors.
  5. Reactor Room — the robot's shield reflects straight throws. Move while throwing to bank discs off the walls.
